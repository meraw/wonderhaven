# Wonderhaven: design notes

## The loop
Explore → meet a villager → hear their problem → gather materials → build a solution with the Starforge Tool → watch the village change → earn Harmony, blueprints and outfits → unlock the next area.

## Hearthglow Village (chapter 1)
| Area | What happens there |
| --- | --- |
| West road and pier | You arrive; Odessa waits at the gate with the Starforge Tool. Renewable trees along the road and in the west grove. |
| Town square | The dry fountain, notice board, lamps. Fills with villagers and flowers once the fountain runs. |
| Orchard Bridge site | River with steep banks. Planks reach two tiles over water from a bank, post or piling. |
| Orchard (north) | Fruit trees, a hidden chest, a dig spot, a free-build garden once the bridge is up. |
| Bram's workshop | Hollow building with a doorway; its roof fades while you are inside or building in it. |
| Mill channel and Sprite Spring | Flowing water for a waterwheel; a pump beside it can feed pipes to the fountain's east inlet. |
| Old quarry (west) | Rocks for stone, metal, and crystals (the glowing blue rock is rich). |
| Castle mound (east) | Lord Percival's post; a chest hides behind the castle. |
| Harbor (south) | Walled by cliffs and a sealed gate (Royal Decree No. 47, lifted at 120 Harmony). Broken dock with old pilings; the ferry. |
| Your cottage | Wardrobe, bed (rest until morning) and your garden plot (grows at 50 Harmony). |

## Villagers
Odessa Quill (master artisan, mentor), Fern Marigold (gardener who talks to plants), Bram Shavings (shy carpenter with extravagant secret designs), Lord Percival Puffington (theatrical royal advisor), Tilly Sprocket (absent-minded inventor), Mabel Crumb (baker vs. squirrels), Captain Wendell Gale (tall-tale ferry captain). Each has a routine, topics, quests, reactions to the village's progress and a friendship gift.

## Quests
Main: The Starforge Tool → Repair the Orchard Bridge → Furnish the Workshop → Restore the Garden Fountain → The Royal Decree → All Aboard! Side: Shiny Stones for the Castle, Squirrels in the Scones, Petals for the Plaza, A Fishy Tale, Lost Starforge Notes, Bright Ideas.

Construction quests check what you built, not one layout: any continuous, supported walkable path counts as a bridge; any arrangement with a workbench, seat, lamp, shelf and one recoloured piece furnishes the workshop; any connected wheel → power → pump → pipe chain that delivers water to the inlet restores the fountain.

## Kingdom Harmony
15 Cozy Corners · 50 A Bigger Garden · 80 Sparkling Lights · 120 Harbor Charter · 160 Starlight Steps · 200 Royal Recognition · 250 Starforge Virtuoso. Harmony comes from quests, building a piece type for the first time, and friendship.
